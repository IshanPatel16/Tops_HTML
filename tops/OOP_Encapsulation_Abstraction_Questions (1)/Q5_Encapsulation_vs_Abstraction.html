Question 5: Encapsulation vs Abstraction

Encapsulation

Encapsulation means keeping data and the methods that work with that data together inside a class, while controlling direct access to the data. For example, in an Instagram-like app, a user's phone number or private profile information can be kept private. Public methods can be used to safely read or update the information.

Abstraction

Abstraction means hiding complicated implementation details and showing only the important features to the user. For example, when we post a story on Instagram, we simply choose a photo or video and press Post. We do not need to know how the app uploads the media, stores it, or distributes it to followers internally.

Main Difference

Encapsulation focuses on protecting and controlling access to data, while abstraction focuses on hiding unnecessary implementation details.

Simple Example

  • Encapsulation: Private profile data is accessed through controlled methods.
  • Abstraction: The Post button hides the complex upload process behind a simple interface.
